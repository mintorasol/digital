Return Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

30-Day Money Back Guarantee

We stand behind every product we sell. If you're not satisfied with your digital software license within the first 30 days of purchase, we'll refund your money—no questions asked!

1. Return Time Window

Customers may request a return or refund within 30 days from the date of purchase. Returns requested after 30 days will not be eligible unless there is a documented technical issue or defect.

  • Return period starts from the order date
  • Proof of purchase (order confirmation email) is required
  • Refund requests must be submitted within the 30-day window

2. Return Eligibility

A purchase is eligible for return if:

  • The license key was never activated or used
  • The software is incompatible with your system (documented)
  • The license key is defective or invalid
  • The product received is not as described
  • You discovered an error in the product information

Not Eligible for Return:

  • License keys that have been activated or registered
  • Used software keys with active subscriptions
  • Products purchased more than 30 days ago
  • Bulk purchases without prior authorization
  • Purchases linked to promotional codes that cannot be refunded

3. How to Request a Return

Step-by-step process:

  1. Contact our support team at support@licensehub.com
  2. Include your order ID and order date in your request
  3. Clearly state the reason for the return
  4. Provide any relevant documentation or error messages
  5. Wait for confirmation of your return request (usually within 24 hours)
  6. Upon approval, follow any additional instructions provided
  7. Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days

4. Refund Processing

  • Refund Method: PayPal account or original payment method
  • Processing Time: 5-7 business days after approval
  • Bank Processing: Additional 1-2 business days may be needed by your bank
  • Partial Refunds: Available only for bulk purchases with documented issues

Note: PayPal refunds are typically faster than bank transfers. You will receive a confirmation email once the refund is processed.

5. Special Circumstances

Technical Issues:

  • If a license key doesn't work, we'll provide a replacement key within 24 hours
  • If the issue persists, a full refund is available regardless of usage

Product Not as Described:

  • If the software doesn't match the product description, return within 30 days
  • Provide screenshots or documentation of the discrepancy

Duplicate Orders:

  • Contact us immediately if you receive duplicate charge
  • We'll process an immediate refund for the duplicate

6. Fraud Prevention

LicenseHub reserves the right to deny refunds if:

  • The request appears to be fraudulent or suspicious
  • The license key(s) were obtained through fraudulent payment
  • The customer has a pattern of abuse (excessive returns)
  • The return request contradicts system logs (showing activation/use)
  • The payment was disputed or charged back previously

7. Digital Product Terms

Please understand that digital software licenses are unique because:

  • They cannot be physically returned or restocked
  • Once activated, they cannot be resold or transferred
  • Downloaded content is for your personal use only
  • License keys are tied to your account and cannot be shared

Therefore, we cannot process returns for activated or used licenses.

8. No Return Shipping Required

Since our products are digital, you don't need to ship anything back! Simply:

  • Submit your return request online
  • We'll deactivate the license key
  • Your refund will be processed

It's that simple and fast!

9. Warranty Information

All our licenses come with a warranty that they will:

  • Be genuine and legally licensed
  • Be valid and not previously used
  • Work with compatible systems as described
  • Be delivered within the stated timeframe

If a license doesn't meet these standards, contact support immediately for a replacement or refund.

10. Policy Changes

LicenseHub reserves the right to modify this return policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting to our website. Your continued use of our service following any such modifications constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Need Help?

Have questions about our return policy? Our support team is here to help!

  • Email: support@licensehub.com
  • Response Time: Within 24 hours
  • Available: 7 days a week

We're committed to making sure you're completely satisfied with your purchase.
