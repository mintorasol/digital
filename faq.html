Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about LicenseHub and our digital products.

How long does delivery take?

Digital license keys are typically delivered within 5-30 minutes of successful payment via email. Please check your spam folder if you don't see the email immediately.

Can I return a license key?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all digital license keys. If you're not satisfied, simply contact our support team at support@licensehub.com within 30 days of purchase.

Are the keys authentic?

Yes! All license keys sold on LicenseHub are 100% genuine and authorized by the software vendors. We guarantee authentic, legitimate software activation.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept secure PayPal payments for all our digital products. PayPal is a trusted payment processor that keeps your financial information safe.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

For bulk orders, please contact our sales team for custom pricing. We work with businesses and organizations of all sizes.

How do I activate my license key?

Instructions for activating your license key are included in the delivery email along with the key. Follow the step-by-step guide provided by the software vendor.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes! We use 256-bit SSL encryption for all data transmission. Your personal and payment information is protected by industry-standard security protocols.

Can I use the same key on multiple computers?

Each license key's usage restrictions are determined by the software vendor. Check your product description or contact support for specific software license terms.

What if I didn't receive my license key?

Please check your spam folder first. If you still haven't received it after 30 minutes, contact our support team with your order ID.

Do you offer technical support for the software?

We assist with license key delivery and account issues. For software-specific technical support, please contact the software vendor's support team.

Still have questions?

Our support team is here to help! Reach out to us anytime.
